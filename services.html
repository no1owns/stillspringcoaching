How I can help

A place to start, wherever you are.

Individual coaching for adults navigating change, neurodivergence, disability, and expectations that don't quite fit.

01

New information

Making sense of an ADHD or autism diagnosis, for yourself or someone you love.

You or someone you love is newly diagnosed, and you're wondering what comes next.

02

Old expectations

Questioning what you “should” do and exploring an approach that actually fits.

Something that used to work no longer feels right, and you're ready to consider a different way.

03

Too much at once

Untangling competing ideas, responsibilities and decisions to move forward.

You have a lot of ideas, questions, or possibilities, and could use help making sense of where to begin.

You don't have to sort it all out before reaching out.

A different kind of coaching conversation.

I take what you're facing seriously, without believing every conversation has to be heavy. We might question an old assumption, explore a practical challenge, or laugh about the absurdity of being human. My job is to help you get clearer about what you want and experiment with ways to move toward it. The goal isn't to hand you someone else's formula. It's to help you discover what fits you.

How sessions work

Sessions are one-on-one, usually by phone. Video calls and local in-person meetings may also be possible by arrangement.

I'm currently offering reduced-rate coaching as I complete my training. We can discuss the details when we connect.

Coaching is not therapy and does not replace mental health care.
