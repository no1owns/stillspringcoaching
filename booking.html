A few common questions

Do I need to have a disability, ADHD, autism or other diagnosis to work with you?

Absolutely not. You don't need a diagnosis or any particular identity to explore coaching. We can talk about what's happening in your life and what kind of support might be useful.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching focuses on exploring your goals, needs, decisions, and everyday challenges. I don't diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions. Coaching can complement therapy, but it doesn't replace it.

How do coaching sessions work?

Sessions are one-on-one, usually by phone. Video calls or local in-person meetings may also be possible by arrangement. We can discuss what would work best during our introductory conversation.

How much does coaching cost?

I'm currently offering reduced-rate coaching while completing my training. Current rates are $75/hour.

Why not just use AI for coaching?

I'm not a purist when it comes to accommodations. If you think AI might be helpful and haven't tried it yet, by all means. I believe there's something different though about the value of human interaction. I've found that talking things through with people has led to deeper insights for me than using AI alone. Also, I can promise confidentiality and no hallucinations where AI cannot.
