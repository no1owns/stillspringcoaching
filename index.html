MIA'S APPROACH

It's not about becoming someone else.

Mia Feldbaum (she/her) · Coach

A lot of people come to coaching thinking they need more willpower, stricter habits, or someone to hold them accountable to expectations that may not work for them in the first place.

I'm interested in a different conversation. One where we take your experiences seriously, question assumptions, and get curious about what actually fits your life.

There's room for practical problem-solving, bigger questions, and even some humor along the way.
