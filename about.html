My Story

From burned out to grounded

For years I was the person who had it all — on paper. A demanding job, a full calendar, and a growing sense that something wasn't quite right. I kept moving faster, hoping to outrun the feeling.

It wasn't until I stopped — really stopped — that I found what I was looking for. Still Spring Coaching grew out of that experience: a conviction that the answers we're looking for don't come from doing more, but from learning to listen to what's already there.

I trained as a [certification] coach and have worked with clients across industries and life stages. What stays the same is the approach: curious, honest, and completely client-led.
