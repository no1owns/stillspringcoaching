About Mia

Meet Mia.

I take what you're facing seriously, without believing every conversation has to be heavy. We might explore an old expectation, work through a practical challenge, or share a laugh along the way.

Portrait to be supplied

Hi, I'm Mia Feldbaum.

I believe disability and neurodivergence are natural parts of human diversity. But navigating our own needs, other people's expectations, and the systems around us can be complicated. I'm interested in both the practical, day-to-day parts of life and the beliefs we carry about how life is supposed to look.

My background includes a master's degree in Cultural Foundations of Education and a Certificate of Advanced Study in Disability Studies. I'm currently completing coach training through The Coach Approach Training Institute and working toward professional credentialing.

“I take you seriously. We can still laugh.”

Mia Feldbaum • Coach in training
